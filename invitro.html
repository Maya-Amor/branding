Case studies / Invitro

Next gen lab grown tea

Visual design concept for Tea Branding

Project Overview

A university project that redefines the essence of tea through biotechnology and precision. The goal was to create a unique tea experience, engineered in laboratory conditions, where every cup is the result of a scientific formula and a deep curiosity for nature.

The visual identity reflects the lab grown innovation behind the product: clean, futuristic aesthetics paired with botanical elegance. Through minimalist design, muted tones, and refined typography.
